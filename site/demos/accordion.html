Shipping questions

How long does delivery take?
Orders inside the EU arrive in 2–4 working days. Everywhere else takes 5–9.
Can I change my address after ordering?
Yes, until the parcel is handed to the courier. Open the order and choose “Edit address”.
Do you ship to PO boxes?
Only with standard post. Express couriers need a street address.
